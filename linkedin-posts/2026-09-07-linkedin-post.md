**Pillar:** Behind-the-Scenes
**Topic:** Canadian-owned and operated since 1996 — what that means

---

1996. No investors, no head office, no five-year exit plan. Just a family that decided to build sports lockers the right way — and never stopped.

Thirty years later, that's still exactly what we are.

I bring this up because I get asked a lot: "Is PlayerStall really still family-owned?" People are surprised. In this industry, that's rare. Most of the suppliers you'll come across have been bought, sold, merged, or absorbed into some larger group at least once. The name on the invoice isn't always the people who answer the phone.

Here's what staying family-owned actually means for you, practically speaking. When you call with a problem — a locker that got damaged, a part that needs replacing five years after installation — you're talking to someone who has a personal stake in fixing it, not a rep reading from a policy manual. Decisions about materials, craftsmanship, and how we honor our lifetime guarantee get made by people who'll still be here next year, and the year after that. Nobody's optimizing us for a sale.

It also means every locker that leaves our shop has our name on it. Literally and personally. That's not something you can fake, and it's not something private equity tends to prioritize.

Thirty years in, I still think that's the whole point.

If you've worked with a small, family-run supplier before — what made that relationship different for you?
