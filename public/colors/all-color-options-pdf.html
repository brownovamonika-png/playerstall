Complete Material Options

Every PlayerStall locker is built from the same 3/4" (19 mm) cabinet-grade plywood. Only the face finish changes: melamine for Walnut, Maple, Black, Light Grey, Dark Grey, Blue, Red, White and Cream, or UV coating for UV Coated Birch. All materials are ENF certified (zero harmful chemicals), water-resistant, and anti-fungal. All locker colors cost the same. Available in any dimension with your choice of finish, color, and hardware.

Essential Wood

UV Coated Birch: UV Grade A

UV coated on both sides of the panel: 3 UV base coats plus 1 clear topcoat, sealed onto a birch veneer face. Made from our 19 mm (3/4") cabinet-grade birch plywood, known for its smooth face, stable veneer core, and natural wood look. Strong, professional-grade lockers that hold up in demanding locker room settings.
