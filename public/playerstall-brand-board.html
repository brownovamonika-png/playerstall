Example 24

Dual logo — PLAYER + script stall

Where Merch / dual-font lockup; tagline also on invoices & homepage

Source Merch reference photo · Adobe Fonts / MyFonts ID · homepage tagline

PLAYEROswald 700 · uppercase · tight tracking (~0.02em) · #0d0d0d
stall (script)Best match: Turbinado (Aerotype / Stephen Miggas) — dry-brush casual script · lowercase · ~#4a4a4a
stall weightLikely Regular or Dry (built-in distress + long t-crossbar)
Why not Great VibesGreat Vibes/Allura = smooth copperplate formal scripts. Merch “stall” is textured dry-brush with a long horizontal t-bar.
TaglineOswald 400 · uppercase · letter-spacing 0.28em–0.42em · #666666
Tagline textEVERY GAME BEGINS HERE
Where to getAdobe Fonts / MyFonts — Turbinado family (incl. Dry styles)
